StyleHub

Online Fashion Marketplace & Design Showcase

Site Name

StyleHub was selected because the name combines "Style" and "Hub." It represents a central place where people can discover fashion designs, clothing items, and creative work.

Site Purpose

StyleHub will provide an online platform where users can explore fashion designs and clothing items, view product descriptions and prices, and discover creative fashion work. Users will be able to filter fashion items by categories such as clothing type, gender, and style. The site will also provide information for users who want to showcase or submit their own fashion designs.

Scenarios

  • What types of clothing and fashion designs are available on StyleHub?
  • Can I filter fashion items by gender, clothing type, or style?
  • How much does a particular fashion item cost?
  • How can I submit or showcase my own fashion design?

Color Schema

Dark Charcoal — #1F1F1F

Used for the header, navigation, headings, and important text.

Warm Off-White — #F5F1EC

Used as the main page background to provide a clean, comfortable appearance.

Warm Bronze — #C08B5C

Used for buttons, links, highlights, borders, and other visual accents.

Typography

Playfair Display

Used for the StyleHub site title, main headings, and important section headings. This serif font gives the site a fashionable and editorial appearance.

Montserrat

Used for body text, navigation, buttons, product information, descriptions, and other interface elements. It provides clear and readable text across different screen sizes.
